## 0. Two-layer structure — critical, easy to confuse

CD 142 has **two separate sets of Articles**, both numbered from 1:

**Outer Decision (3 articles):**
| Art | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Imposition of Top-up Tax per the Annexure |
| **2** | **"This Decision shall apply to the Fiscal Years beginning on or after 1 January 2025."** ← the applicability gate used in Module 4 |
| 3 | Takes effect 1 Jan 2025, published in Official Gazette |

**Annexure (18 articles)** — all substantive rules. The Annexure's own Article 2 is the *Charging Provision*, a completely different article from the outer Decision's Article 2. **Always specify which layer when citing Article 2.**

## Article 5 — ETR and Top-up Tax  ← **the payoff module**
### 5.1 ETR determination
- ETR = Adjusted Covered Taxes ÷ Net Pillar Two Income (UAE jurisdictional blending)
- 5.1.2 Net Pillar Two Income definition
- 5.1.3 Investment Entity exclusion from this computation

### 5.2 Top-up Tax
- 5.2.1 **Top-up Tax Percentage = positive difference between 15% (Minimum Rate) and ETR**
- 5.2.2 Excess Profit = Net Pillar Two Income − SBIE
- 5.2.3 Top-up Tax = (Top-up Tax % × Excess Profit) + Additional Current Top-up Tax − QDMTT
- 5.2.4/5.2.5 Allocation among Constituent Entities where no DDFE appointed
- 5.2.6 Where ETR computed differently

### 5.3 Substance-based Income Exclusion (SBIE) — 13 sub-articles
- **5.3.3 Payroll carve-out = 5% of Eligible Payroll Costs**
- **5.3.4 Tangible asset carve-out = 5% of carrying value of Eligible Tangible Assets**
  - Eligible Tangible Assets: (a) PP&E in UAE, (b) natural resources in UAE, (c) lessee right-of-use, (d) licences/similar for immovable property use
- 5.3.5–5.3.13: carrying value computation, exclusions (shipping income), partial claims, PE/flow-through attribution

### 5.4 Additional Current Top-up Tax (5.4.1–5.4.2)
Recalculation of prior-year ETR/Top-up Tax.

### 5.5 De minimis exclusion — **Annual Election**
Top-up Tax deemed **zero** if BOTH:
- **(a) Average Pillar Two Revenue < EUR 10 million**, AND
- **(b) Average Pillar Two Income or Loss is a loss, or < EUR 1 million**

Average = current + 2 preceding FYs (5.5.2). Does not apply to Investment Entities (5.5.4).

### 5.6 Minority-Owned Constituent Entities (5.6.1–5.6.2)
Separate ETR/Top-up Tax computation for Minority-Owned Subgroups.

## Article 8 — Filing and Safe Harbours
### 8.1 Top-up Tax Return
- **Deadline: 15 months after last day of Reporting Fiscal Year; 18 months for the first Transition Year**

### 8.2 Safe Harbours — **three distinct mechanisms**
- **8.2.1 Transitional CbCR Safe Harbour** — transitional only (FYs beginning before 1 Jan 2027, ending before 1 July 2028). Three alternative tests: De Minimis / Simplified ETR ≥ Transition Rate / Routine Profits. Adjustment list at 8.2.1.3, disqualifying conditions at 8.2.1.4
- **8.2.2 Simplified Calculations Safe Harbour** — **permanent**, separate machinery. Three tests (Routine Profits / De Minimis / ETR), uses Simplified Income/Revenue/Tax Calculations and Non-material Constituent Entity concept
- **8.2.3 Disapplication** — FTA clawback, 36-month window

Top-up Tax **reduced to zero** if: (a) Constituent Entities in ≤6 Jurisdictions, AND (b) sum of Net Book Values of Tangible Assets outside the reference Jurisdiction ≤ **EUR 50 million** — provided no UAE ownership interests held by a Parent Entity subject to a Qualified IIR elsewhere.
