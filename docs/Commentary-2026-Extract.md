## ⚠️ CRITICAL DIVERGENCE — UAE's Transition Period/Rate differs from OECD's

This must not be got wrong in any Safe Harbour build. **Use the UAE figures.**

| | **CD 142 (UAE — USE THIS)** | OECD Commentary (generic) |
|---|---|---|
| **Transition Period** | FYs beginning **before 1 Jan 2027** and ending **before 1 July 2028** | FYs beginning on or before 31/12/2027, not ending after 30/6/2029 |
| **Transition Rate** | 16% (FYs beginning 2025)<br>17% (FYs beginning 2026) | 15% (2023, 2024)<br>16% (2025)<br>17% (2026, 2027) |

**Why they differ:** the OECD text covers jurisdictions that started in 2023/2024. The UAE's regime begins 1 January 2025, so CD 142 omits the 15% tier entirely and shortens the window. **A tool using the OECD dates/rates would produce wrong answers for UAE users.** Cite CD 142 Art 8.2 definitions, not the Commentary's, for these two terms.

## 3. Article 3.2 (Income adjustments) — Commentary structure

The Commentary elaborates each of CD 142 Art 3.2.1's eleven adjustments in depth. Sub-topics identified within the first portion alone:
Covered Taxes · Deferred Tax Asset · **Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Taxes** · GloBE Taxes · Disqualified Refundable Imputation Taxes · Short-term Portfolio Shareholding · Dividends/distributions received · Included vs Excluded Dividend · Changes in fair value · Equity method accounting · Gains/losses on disposition · Equity Investment Inclusion Election · Treatment of tax credits derived through a Tax Transparent Entity

**At 160k characters (~40 pages), this is the single largest article-level commentary.** Any Module 5 (Pillar Two Income) build needs a dedicated extraction pass on this section alone — comparable in effort to reading a whole FTA guide.

## 5. Transitional CbCR Safe Harbour — operative content (Commentary Annex A Ch. 1 + CD 142 Art 8.2.1)

**Three alternative tests** (meeting any one ⇒ UAE Top-up Tax deemed zero):
- **(a) De Minimis:** Total Revenue < EUR 10m AND Profit (Loss) before Income Tax < EUR 1m in the UAE, per Qualified CbC Report
- **(b) Simplified ETR:** Simplified ETR ≥ Transition Rate (UAE: 16% FY2025 / 17% FY2026)
- **(c) Routine Profits:** Profit (Loss) before Income Tax ≤ SBIE amount computed under CD 142 Arts 5.3 and 9.2

**Definitions:**
- *Simplified Covered Taxes* = jurisdiction's income tax expense per Qualified Financial Statements, after eliminating non-Covered Taxes and uncertain tax positions
- *Simplified ETR* = Simplified Covered Taxes ÷ Profit (Loss) before Income Tax per Qualified CbC Report

**Adjustments required (CD 142 Art 8.2.1.3) — six items:**
(a) Flow-through/Deductible Dividend Regime UPE reduction for Qualified Person ownership · (b) Net Unrealised Fair Value Loss excluded if > EUR 50m in UAE · (c) Investment Entity figures reflected only in direct CE-owners' jurisdictions · (d) Hybrid Arbitrage Arrangements entered after **15 December 2022** (Deduction Non-inclusion, Duplicate Loss, Duplicate Tax Recognition) · (e) uncertain tax positions removed from income tax expense · (f) PE loss treatment

**JV treatment (Art 8.2.1.2):** JVs and JV Subsidiaries treated as a separate MNE Group, using Qualified Financial Statements figures.

## 6. Permanent Safe Harbours (Commentary Annex A Ch. 2) — maps to CD 142 Art 8.2.2

Four sections, of which the **Simplified ETR Safe Harbour** is by far the largest (~58 pages):
- Simplified Jurisdictional ETR (Tested Jurisdiction, Source of Information)
- Simplified Income or Loss (Basic adjustments to JPBT · Industry adjustments · Conditional adjustments · Optional adjustments)
- Simplified Taxes (JITE · basic adjustments · negative taxes in loss years · optional adjustments · **Transition Year rules** · tax adjustments after year end)
- Simplified Treatment of Cross-border Income & Taxes (incl. transfer pricing adjustments)
- Tax-Neutral Entities (Tax Transparent Entities · Investment Entity Tax Transparency Election)
- **Eligibility Restrictions** — incl. entry/re-entry criteria and **four integrity principles**: matching · full allocation · single expense and loss · single tax

Also covers **Non-material Constituent Entity (NMCE)** simplified calculations and the **Substance-based Tax Incentive (SBTI) Safe Harbour** — the latter not obviously mapped to a CD 142 provision; needs checking before use.
